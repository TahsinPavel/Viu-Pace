# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Content creators, video production teams, and modern product teams who need a unified workspace to monitor velocity, automate content distribution, and analyze YouTube channel performance alongside real-time SaaS operations.

## Product Purpose
ViuPace simplifies and accelerates execution by combining real-time SaaS pacing and telemetry with automated YouTube video workflows and analytics, allowing teams to track performance metrics and deliver content faster without context switching.

## Positioning
Unified execution workspace integrating engineering velocity tracking and automated YouTube media/content operations into a single clean, high-efficiency dashboard.

## Operating Context
Daily content operations, sprint velocity tracking, YouTube channel analytics review, video batch workflows, and team performance monitoring across web browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Frontend**: Next.js 16+ (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Shadcn UI components.
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python), Uvicorn, SQLAlchemy async engine, `asyncpg`.
- **Database**: Serverless Neon PostgreSQL.
- **Dashboard UI**: Minimalist white workspace with dedicated left panel and YouTube integration views.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: ViuPace
- **Visual Tone**: Clean, minimal, high-contrast, professional white aesthetic.

## Product Principles
1. **Uncluttered & Focused**: Pure white, distraction-free interface prioritizing clarity and scanning speed.
2. **Real-Time Visibility**: Instant feedback on velocity metrics, YouTube channel stats, and workflow operations.
3. **Seamless Integration**: Cohesive connection between Next.js frontend actions and FastAPI async backend operations.
